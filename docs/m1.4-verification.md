# M1.4 map verification

This record separates checks that ran from the interactive review still needed
to close the M1.4 acceptance gate.

## Automated checks — 2026-10-28

From the repository root, `dotnet restore Interregnum.sln` and
`dotnet build Interregnum.sln --no-restore` completed successfully. The build
reported zero warnings and zero errors. `dotnet test Interregnum.sln --no-build`
passed all 37 tests with no failures or skips.

Godot 4.7.2 .NET started the main scene in headless mode and completed three
frames without project, scene, or C# errors. The sandbox prevented Godot from
opening its user log and Windows certificate store; these environment warnings
did not stop the scene. A headless movie capture reproduced the known Godot
dummy-renderer crash documented in `foundation-verification.md`, so it did not
provide visual evidence and its temporary output was removed.

## Terrain correction checks — 2026-10-29

After replacing the procedural relief marks with the Natural Earth I terrain
extract and removing outlines around source country polygons, the solution
built with zero warnings and errors and all 37 tests passed. A three-frame
headless main-scene launch loaded the terrain without project, scene, C#, or
resource errors; only the known sandbox log and certificate-store warnings
remained.

A two-frame Windows renderer capture at 1440x900 confirmed at 1.0x that physical
relief and land-cover variation are visible beneath translucent ownership,
modern country borders are absent, and the existing fictional province borders,
labels, rivers, and side panel remain visible. The temporary capture was removed
after inspection. Higher zoom bands and interactive controls still require the
manual checks below.

Following review feedback, the initial 960x780 1:50m extract was replaced by a
1920x1560 1:10m extract. Its transparency is now generated from the existing
vector land geometry rather than the raster water color. A new 1440x900 Windows
renderer capture confirmed that Britain remains separate from mainland Europe,
Hispania remains separate from North Africa, the coastline is substantially
cleaner, and relief/land-cover detail is sharper at 1.0x. Interactive inspection
at maximum zoom remains required because a rendered overview cannot establish
high-zoom texture quality or input behavior.

## Interactive acceptance checklist

Run the game at its default 1440x900 window size and resize it through the
supported desktop range. At each of the following zoom bands, check the listed
behavior:

- 1.0x: Western Europe context is readable, terrain remains dominant beneath
  ownership, all province labels fit, and thin internal borders do not dominate.
- 1.7x: settlements, river names, and extra relief marks appear without labels
  scaling up or obscuring most of a province.
- 2.35x: army strength markers appear in occupied provinces and remain legible.
- 6.0x: the cursor anchor remains stable, panning reaches every map edge, labels
  stay at a readable screen-space size, and province selection matches the
  visible polygon.

Also verify mouse-wheel zoom, middle-drag pan, +/- zoom, arrow/WASD pan, 0/Home
reset, tax policy changes, rejected foreign-province orders, End Turn feedback,
the detailed turn tooltip, live loyalty/rebellion overlays, and scrolling of the
side panel in a shorter window.

## Known visual limitations

- Physical relief and vegetation use a subdued Natural Earth I raster. It is
  modern generalized land-cover reference data, not reconstructed historical
  land cover for 480 AD.
- Rivers and relief paths are generalized for orientation at province scale.
- Province geometry remains fictionalized and clipped to a modern physical
  coastline; it must not be presented as a historical border reconstruction.
- Final texture transitions, animation, audio, broad accessibility tuning, and
  release art direction remain Milestone 5 work.

Map source and presentation provenance are recorded in
`src/Interregnum.Game/Data/MAP-DATA.md`.
