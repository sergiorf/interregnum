# M1.4 detailed terrain and zoom plan

**Status:** Proposed M1.4 follow-up; implementation requires approval of the
regional bounds, source/licensing choices, zoom cap, and asset budgets below.

## 1. Purpose

Make the campaign map feel physically larger by allowing deeper zoom to reveal
meaningful terrain and gameplay detail. Increasing the camera limit alone is not
acceptable: each added zoom band must expose useful information without simply
enlarging pixels, labels, borders, or coarse province geometry.

This is a bounded presentation change within M1.4. Terrain remains decorative
and non-authoritative. `Interregnum.Core` continues to own all gameplay state
and rules; the Godot client owns terrain assets, camera behavior, labels, hit
testing, and overlays.

## 2. Current constraints

- The Western Europe terrain is 1920x1560 pixels.
- Gaul occupies approximately 900x600 pixels of that image.
- The camera currently supports 1x through 6x zoom.
- Each of the 12 scenario provinces has only eight polygon vertices.
- A larger zoom limit would therefore magnify both terrain pixels and angular
  province boundaries unless terrain and geometry improve together.

## 3. Recommended target

Adopt a 10x maximum zoom initially. Consider 12x only after the 10x acceptance
review proves that additional zoom would reveal real detail.

| Zoom | Terrain and interaction level |
| --- | --- |
| 1x-2x | Western Europe overview, major relief, ownership, abbreviated or primary labels |
| 2x-4x | High-resolution Gaul terrain, forests/plains variation, major rivers and settlements |
| 4x-7x | Native regional terrain detail, minor relief, armies and full settlement labels |
| 7x-10x | Local terrain texture, minor physical features and precise province interaction |

Recommended detailed-region bounds are approximately 6 degrees west to
11 degrees east and 41 to 53 degrees north. This covers the playable campaign
plus the Rhine frontier, northern Italy, northern Iberia, southern Britannia,
and enough surrounding context for panning.

Western Europe outside this region keeps the overview terrain. Deep detail is
not generated for non-interactive Scandinavia, North Africa, or eastern Europe.
There is no runtime network access; all required assets ship with the game.

## 4. Proposed physical sources

Use separate sources for separate physical concepts:

- **Land, coastline, rivers, and lakes:** Natural Earth 1:10m physical vectors,
  using physical land/coastline products rather than Admin 0 country polygons.
  Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/
- **Elevation:** Copernicus DEM GLO-30 where access, redistribution, and required
  attribution are approved; GLO-90 is the fallback.
  Source: https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM
- **Land-cover reference:** ESA WorldCover 2021 at 10m, under CC BY 4.0.
  Source: https://esa-worldcover.org/en/data-access

Elevation and coastline may be treated as physical reference. Modern land-cover
must be generalized into art direction, not represented as reconstructed 480 AD
vegetation. In particular, modern cropland must not be shown literally as
historical agriculture. Tree, grassland, and shrub classes may guide restrained
forest and open-land masks with the required attribution.

Before downloading or committing derived assets, record exact versions,
checksums, access terms, redistribution rights, required notices, and extraction
dates in `src/Interregnum.Game/Data/MAP-DATA.md`.

## 5. Offline asset pipeline

Create a reproducible development-time tool under `tools/map/`. It must not run
when the game launches and must not introduce a runtime dependency.

The pipeline should:

1. Download or locate approved source files in a gitignored working directory.
2. Verify source versions and checksums.
3. Crop sources to the approved regional bounds.
4. Reproject them into the map's current longitude/latitude coordinate space.
5. Generate hillshade from elevation using documented parameters.
6. Convert land-cover classes into a small restrained palette: forest, open
   plain, Mediterranean open land, mountain rock, high-elevation snow, and
   water.
7. Blend elevation, hillshade, and generalized land cover.
8. Mask land with the 1:10m physical land polygon.
9. Cut the result into padded terrain tiles.
10. Emit a manifest containing bounds, projection, tile size, zoom range,
    source versions, paths, and checksums.

Large source rasters do not belong in the repository. Commit only the processed
game assets, the manifest, generation tool/instructions, and provenance record.
Any required preprocessing dependency must be justified and remain outside the
shipped game.

## 6. Tile format and budgets

Use 512x512 visible pixels per tile with a two-pixel duplicated gutter around
each tile. The gutter prevents filtering seams.

The initial target regional surface is approximately 6144x4608 effective
pixels, or roughly 12 by 9 tiles before omitting all-water tiles. The exact
height may change when the final geographic bounds are approved.

The existing 1920x1560 terrain remains the overview layer. The detailed layer
uses lossless Godot texture import with mipmaps enabled so it can transition
cleanly from overview scale.

Provisional budgets requiring approval:

- No more than 80 MB of committed processed terrain assets.
- No more than 64 MB of terrain GPU memory during ordinary play.
- A runtime cache of 24-32 detailed tiles, adjusted using measurements.
- No single global high-resolution texture and no runtime downloads.

The tile manifest should contain at least:

```json
{
  "version": 1,
  "projection": "EPSG:4326",
  "bounds": {
    "west": -6,
    "east": 11,
    "south": 41,
    "north": 53
  },
  "tileSize": 512,
  "gutter": 2,
  "columns": 12,
  "rows": 9,
  "minimumZoom": 2.0,
  "maximumZoom": 10.0
}
```

The manifest is presentation metadata. It must never enter `WorldState` or
affect terrain legality, movement, economy, or other rules.

## 7. Godot rendering design

Keep `ProvinceMap` responsible for camera input, selection, and gameplay
overlays, but move terrain loading and drawing into one focused presentation
component:

```text
ProvinceMap
 |-- camera, selection and gameplay overlays
 `-- TerrainTileLayer
     |-- overview texture
     |-- tile manifest
     |-- visible-tile calculation
     `-- bounded texture cache
```

`TerrainTileLayer` should:

- Convert the current pan and zoom into a visible geographic rectangle.
- Load only detailed tiles intersecting that rectangle.
- Preload one surrounding tile ring to avoid visible pop-in during panning.
- Keep a bounded least-recently-used cache and release evicted textures.
- Crossfade overview and detailed terrain across a narrow 2x-2.5x transition.
- Draw tiles below province ownership, selection, unrest, labels, settlements,
  armies, and other gameplay overlays.
- Fail gracefully to the overview texture if a tile is absent or invalid.
- Never access the network or simulation rules.

Godot texture filtering should be selected explicitly. Linear filtering with
mipmaps is the starting choice for non-pixel-art terrain; verify it against
seams and high-zoom softness rather than relying on defaults.

## 8. Camera behavior

Change the camera limit only after the detailed tile proof renders correctly.

- Initial maximum zoom: 10x.
- Mouse-wheel multiplier: approximately 1.15 for finer control.
- Keyboard/button multiplier: approximately 1.25.
- Preserve cursor-centered zoom and bounded panning.
- Keep 0/Home as the 1x reset.
- Add a focus-selected-province action only if interaction testing shows it is
  useful; do not add it merely as camera scaffolding.
- Never expose empty space outside map bounds.
- Keep borders, labels, settlements, and army markers at controlled screen-space
  sizes rather than multiplying them with terrain scale.

## 9. Province geometry

Ten-times zoom is not acceptable with eight-point province polygons. Refine the
presentation geometry without changing province IDs, ownership, adjacency, or
simulation rules.

- Preserve the fictional political layout; do not claim historical precision.
- Add detail to coastal and shared internal boundaries.
- Make adjacent provinces use identical shared-edge coordinate sequences.
- Clip coastal provinces against the approved 1:10m land mask.
- Target approximately 24-60 points per province according to shape.
- Use the same final polygons for rendering and hit testing.
- Keep approved scenario adjacency authoritative; never infer it from pixels or
  polygon contact.

Do not smooth each province independently. Independent smoothing can create
gaps and overlaps between neighbors. Shared edges must be authored or processed
together.

## 10. Progressive detail rules

Provisional detail thresholds:

- **1x:** abbreviated province labels and major geographic context.
- **1.7x:** full province names, settlements, and major rivers.
- **2.5x:** detailed terrain tiles replace the overview progressively.
- **3.5x:** forest texture and secondary relief become clearer.
- **4.5x:** army and settlement presentation reaches full detail.
- **6x:** minor rivers and regional physical labels appear.
- **8x:** local terrain decoration appears.
- **10x:** maximum detail without further label or border enlargement.

Any forest symbols or local decoration must derive from the generalized masks,
remain visually subordinate to gameplay, and avoid implying exact historical
forest boundaries.

## 11. Verification and acceptance

### Automated asset checks

Add checks that verify:

- The manifest schema and geographic bounds are valid.
- Every referenced tile exists and has the expected dimensions and gutter.
- Tile coordinates cover the declared region without gaps or overlaps.
- Missing all-water tiles are declared and handled intentionally.
- No modern political-border layer is included.
- Asset size remains within the approved repository budget.
- Source/provenance fields are present.
- The terrain coordinate transform agrees with province/map coordinates.

These are presentation-content checks and must not introduce terrain into Core
gameplay rules.

### Interactive Godot checks

At 1440x900 and a smaller supported window, inspect 1x, 2x, 4x, 6x, 8x, and 10x:

- Britain remains separate from mainland Europe and Hispania remains separate
  from North Africa.
- No modern country borders appear.
- No tile seams, missing tiles, obvious LOD pops, or brightness discontinuities
  appear during zoom or pan.
- Mountains, forests, rivers, settlements, and other useful details become
  progressively clearer.
- Province colors remain translucent and readable over every terrain family.
- Province selection agrees with the visible polygon at every zoom.
- Borders and labels remain readable without dominating province interiors.
- Rapid pan and zoom do not cause persistent stutter.
- Tax changes, rejected actions, End Turn, reports, loyalty, and rebellion
  feedback remain functional.

### Provisional performance targets

- Smooth 60 FPS panning on the development machine after nearby tiles load.
- No recurring frame spike above 16-20 ms after the cache is warm.
- No more than a one-frame hitch when entering the detailed zoom range.
- Terrain GPU memory and committed assets remain within approved budgets.

Performance evidence is diagnostic, not proof of visual quality. Record the
test machine, window size, import settings, asset size, and observed limitations.

## 12. Work packages and gates

### T1 - Approve scope and data

- Approve 10x as the initial maximum.
- Approve the regional bounds and overview/detailed split.
- Review source access, redistribution, attribution, and historical caveats.
- Approve repository and runtime memory budgets.

**Gate:** No large source download or full tile generation begins before these
decisions are recorded.

### T2 - Physical geography and pipeline

- Replace the remaining Admin 0-derived land mask with Natural Earth physical
  land/coastline data.
- Implement the reproducible crop, blend, mask, tile, manifest, and checksum
  pipeline.
- Document exact inputs and processing parameters.

**Gate:** A deterministic rerun produces the same proof assets and manifest.

### T3 - Four-tile central Gaul proof

- Generate a small representative proof containing plains, forest, mountains,
  rivers, coastline where feasible, and ownership overlays.
- Implement the manifest reader, visible-tile selection, padded drawing, cache,
  overview fallback, and crossfade.

**Gate:** The proof has no seams or obvious transition artifacts and stays
within measured memory/frame-time expectations.

### T4 - Regional terrain and camera

- Generate the complete approved regional tile set.
- Raise the maximum zoom to 10x.
- Apply the progressive terrain, label, settlement, and army thresholds.
- Tune filtering, preloading, cache size, and crossfade using measurements.

**Gate:** Every zoom level reveals useful detail and rapid camera movement
remains responsive.

### T5 - Province geometry and selection

- Refine shared and coastal province geometry.
- Preserve scenario adjacency and fictionalization warnings.
- Verify rendering and hit testing against the same final polygons.

**Gate:** No visible gaps/overlaps exist and selection matches visible borders
at 1x through 10x.

### T6 - Acceptance and handoff

- Run asset validation, solution build/tests, Godot launch, and the interactive
  zoom matrix.
- Record visual, performance, provenance, and historical limitations.
- Fix M1.4 acceptance defects only; defer final texture artistry, animation,
  audio, and broad content work to M5.

**Gate:** Stop and review M1.4 before beginning M1.5 recruitment and movement.

## 13. Explicit non-goals

- Terrain-dependent movement, economy, combat, or other Core rules.
- A tactical-scale map or individually modeled trees and buildings.
- Runtime web maps, streaming services, or account-dependent downloads.
- Detailed terrain for the entire Western Europe overview.
- Claims of reconstructed 480 AD vegetation or historically exact provinces.
- Final M5 art direction, animation, audio, or release-wide performance polish.

## 14. Decisions requiring approval

1. Is 10x the approved initial cap, with 12x considered only after review?
2. Are the proposed 6 W to 11 E and 41 N to 53 N regional bounds sufficient?
3. May the project use Copernicus DEM and ESA WorldCover derived assets with
   their required attribution and modern-data caveats?
4. Are 80 MB committed terrain and 64 MB runtime terrain memory acceptable
   provisional budgets?
5. Is the four-tile central Gaul proof the required gate before generating the
   complete regional asset set?
